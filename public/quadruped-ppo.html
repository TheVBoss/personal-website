Quadruped Locomotion
with PPO.

I trained a simulated quadruped robot using Proximal Policy Optimisation (PPO) in MuJoCo. I experimented with Domain Randomisation and Friction Conditioned PPO to see how walking changes across ground friction conditions, then used statistical analysis to make sense of the results.
